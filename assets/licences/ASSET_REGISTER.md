**Inspected and rejected (not used anywhere):** everything bundled with SoccerDribble (`FOOTBALL-RALISM-LAB/SoccerDribble`). The project has no licence from its author (a university course project); an MIT text was pasted into its README by someone else. It bundles a Nintendo sound effect, real club shirts and a World Cup ball texture. Its models, textures, sounds and code are not copied; see `docs/ASSET_AND_LICENCE_REGISTER.md`. Ara 3D Gen's full character exports (such as the 29.8 MB `Kofi_Mensah.glb`) and the 54 raw athlete exports are not shipped; only the optimised files above are (see `docs/PLAYER_STUDIO_INTEGRATION_AUDIT.md`). Since 2026-11-28 the game draws every footballer as an athlete; the four close-up bodies stay in `public/models/bodies/` but are no longer loaded.

**Generated in code (no third-party files):** textures, stadium, crowd, ball, faces, all sound
(Web Audio synthesis in `public/sound.js`), commentary, and every name of a player, club,
competition and sponsor.
