# Player Studio — user guide

Open the **Player Studio** from the game's menu (**Game → Player Studio**), or go to `/studio/`. It
works offline, like the game. Everything you see in the Studio is drawn by the match renderer, so a
footballer looks the same here as on the pitch.

## Create
* **Describe him.** Type a description and press **Generate footballer**. For example: "a
  17-year-old Nigerian striker, 183 cm, athletic build, dark skin, short curly low fade, brown eyes,
  broad shoulders, strong legs, clean-shaven, wearing a red and white kit".
* **What it understands:**
  * age, height (cm or feet) and weight;
  * nation and position;
  * skin;
  * face shape, jaw, chin, cheekbones, nose, lips, ears, eyes and brows;
  * hair style and colour;
  * facial hair;
  * build, shoulders, chest, legs and arms;
  * kit colours.

  Anything it does not mention is filled in sensibly for his nation and position.
* **Variations.** Tick the features to keep (skin, face, eyes, hair, beard, body, kit) and press
  **Generate a variation** to change the rest.

## Appearance
Every control changes the 3D model at once:
* **Face:** skin tone, face shape, forehead, jaw, chin, cheekbones, nose width, eyes, eye colour,
  eyebrows, lips and ears.
* **Hair:** style, colour and hairline; facial hair.
* **Body:** height, weight, muscle, shoulders, chest, waist, arm length and leg length.

**Undo and Redo** are at the top.

## Kits
* Design your club's **home, away, third and goalkeeper** kits: shirt, second colour, shorts,
  socks and pattern.
* The player in the Studio wears the kit you are editing, with your crest, your shirt sponsor and
  his name and number.
* Press **Save kits for my club**: your team wears them from the next match. When colours clash
  with the opponents, the away or third kit is chosen automatically.

## Animation
Watch him walk, run, sprint, pass, shoot, slide-tackle, block, head, jump, celebrate, and make a
goalkeeper's set, dives and gather, at any speed.

## Library
* **Save** keeps a character.
* The **Library** tab lets you search, open, duplicate and delete (with confirmation).
* **Export .json** saves his profile. **Export .glb** saves a 3D file with his model, skeleton,
  animations and profile.
* **Import** accepts either file back. A .glb must be one exported by the Studio.

## Use in your career
* Pick one of your players and press **Give him this look**. **Use his own look** changes him back.
* Only his appearance changes. His ratings, contract, club, statistics and history are never
  touched.
* The look stays with him after transfers and in every save.
* Every player, including academy players, already has a permanent look of his own; the Studio is
  optional.
