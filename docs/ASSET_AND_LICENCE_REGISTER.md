## Rules applied

* Only licences that allow commercial use without payment are accepted; a licence that cannot be
  verified means the asset is not shipped.
* A row goes into the register before an asset is used; the MIT notice travels with any MIT material.
* No real clubs' crests, kits or players; no paid or non-commercial assets.

## 2026-11-27

No new third-party files. The match board, player cards, camera, ball and athletes on the pitch use
only the game's own code and the already registered close-up bodies. Dream League Soccer was a visual
reference for the cards and the substitution screen; none of its graphics, models or code were used.

## 2026-11-28

Taken into the game: the **athletes**, registered in `assets/licences/ASSET_REGISTER.md` before use. 54
exports from Ara 3D Gen (commit `76d4e13`), of one neutral footballer and of single MakeHuman modifiers,
became one base footballer with 37 shape targets at two levels of detail, 11 hairstyles, and generated
beard and glove layers.

* MakeHuman data (base mesh, modifiers, skins, eyes, brows, lashes, shoes06, and the eight MakeHuman
  hairstyles) is CC0 1.0 under Ara's asset notice, which names hair and proxy meshes.
* The kit and the cornrows, mohawk and buzz styles are generated by Ara's MIT code. Exports may be used
  commercially without attribution.
* The raw exports stay in `FOOTBALL-RALISM-LAB/outputs/gaffer-athletes/` (not in git). No CMU
  motion-capture data was used: the exports carry no clips.
* Dream League Soccer remained a visual reference only.
* No audio files were added: all match sound is still synthesised in `public/sound.js`.
